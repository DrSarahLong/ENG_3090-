The courseWhat ENG 3090 is

This course introduces the foundational theories and methods of rhetoric and technical communication by having you do the real thing: design and carry out your own mixed-methods research study, then write it up as a professional IMRAD report you could hand to an employer or a graduate program.

We work all semester through one shared example, the sustainability claims on food labels (natural, organic, local, regenerative), and one guiding lens: who do these documents serve, who do they mislead, and whose voice is missing? The food label is our common reference point, but you'll choose your own topic to research across the semester.

By the end, you will have built:

  • A researchable question inside a real debate
  • A literature review of what's known
  • A qualitative discourse & content analysis
  • An ethical, IRB-informed survey or interview study
  • Charts and evidence-tested findings
  • A full IMRAD report and a five-minute talk
