WorkNet

The gap

The unanswered question your sources leave open. Often a missing voice. That gap is the reason your whole study exists.

Group your five into two or three themes. What connects them?

A list reads: one study says shoppers trust "organic," another says "natural" has no legal rule, a third looked at "local." A conversation reads: they agree shoppers trust eco-labels, but the words carry very different rules, and nobody asked the people who grow the food. The strongest gaps often name a voice left out of the frame.

Where do your sources agree? Where do they clash? What's missing?

Sort them, then look for the silence in the conversation.
