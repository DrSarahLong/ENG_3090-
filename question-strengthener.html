ENG 3090 · Week 2

Research Question Strengthener

Run your question through the six things a strong research question does. You do the judging; the tool just keeps score and shows you where to work next.

How it works. Type your research question so you can see it while you work. Then read each of the six checks below and, for each one, tick the boxes that are honestly true of your question. The tool tallies your answers and shows which checks are solid and which need work. It does not read your question for you, that judgment is yours, and it's the whole skill. Revise your question, re-tick the boxes, and watch it get stronger. Everything stays on this computer.

Strong questions often open: How do… · Which… · To what extent… · In what ways…  (they invite findings, not just yes/no).

🎉 All six check out. By your own honest reading, this question is scoped, answerable, and worth doing. Copy it into Entry 2a of your notebook and get ready to read it to the class.

The six checks adapt the widely used FINER criteria for a good research question, from Ratan, Anand & Ratan, “Formulation of Research Question, Stepwise Approach” (Journal of the Indian Association of Pediatric Surgeons, 2019). Read it free (open access).
