Sample & population

The population is everyone your question is about. The sample is the slice you actually reach, standing in for the whole.

Convenience sampling

Recruiting whoever is easy to reach. Common and fine, but easy-to-reach usually means people like you.

Selection bias

When your sampling method quietly favors one kind of person (the farmers'-market problem), or you pick who "looks right" (cherry-picking).

Sample size

Aim for a few dozen responses on a survey, or five to eight deep interviews. Enough to see a pattern instead of one anecdote.

If you only ask people like you, you studied your friends, not shoppers.
