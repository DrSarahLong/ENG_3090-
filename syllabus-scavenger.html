What we mean by a "social justice lens"

Technical writing is never neutral. Every document, form, chart, or interface makes choices about who can understand it, who it leaves out, and who carries the risk when it fails.

A social justice lens just means we keep asking those questions while we research and write: Who holds power here? Who's served? Whose voice is missing? It's not about scoring political points, it's about noticing that design has real consequences for real people, and doing something about it.

Go deeper (free and open, no login, no paywall):

  • Technical Communication, Social Justice, and Collective Access, an open textbook chapter. A plain-language intro written for students: how the documents we design include or exclude people, and why access itself is a justice issue.
  • Technical Communication and Social Justice, a free, open-access journal. Browse it to see what real work in this area looks like. No subscription needed.
